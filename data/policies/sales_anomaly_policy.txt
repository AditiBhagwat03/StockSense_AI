POLICY ID: ANM-001
TITLE: Sales Change Detection

Compare the current 30-day sales period with the
previous 30-day period.

A change of 30 percent or more in either direction
should be flagged for investigation.

Increase of 30 percent or more:
SALES SPIKE

Decrease of 30 percent or more:
SALES DROP

The system should report the measured change and
the underlying sales values.

A sales anomaly does not establish its cause.

The system must not invent explanations when the
available data does not contain causal information.
