POLICY ID: REL-001
TITLE: Inter-Store Stock Review

When a store has high stock-out risk for a product,
the system may check other stores for available stock.

If another store has sufficient inventory and lower
sales demand, inter-store reallocation may be suggested
for human review.

The system must show:

1. Store with the shortage
2. Current stock at that store
3. Sales rate at that store
4. Candidate source store
5. Available stock at the source store
6. Reason for the recommendation

The system must not claim that stock has been moved.
Any reallocation recommendation requires human approval.
