POLICY ID: OVS-001
TITLE: Overstock Identification

A product may be flagged for overstock review when
its inventory coverage exceeds 21 weeks based on
recent average weekly sales.

Products with zero sales during the review period
should also be flagged for inventory review when
stock remains available.

An overstock flag does not automatically mean that the
product should be discounted.

The system should recommend reviewing purchasing,
inventory allocation, or promotional options rather than
automatically selecting a discount.
