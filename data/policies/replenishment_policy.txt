POLICY ID: REP-001
TITLE: Replenishment Review

A product should be reviewed for replenishment when
projected stock coverage is below the supplier lead time.

Priority should increase when the product is already
classified as high stock-out risk.

The recommended replenishment quantity should consider:

1. Current stock
2. Recent average sales
3. Target stock level
4. Supplier lead time

The system must present the assumptions used to
calculate any suggested replenishment quantity.

The system must not claim that a purchase order has
been placed or that stock will definitely arrive.
