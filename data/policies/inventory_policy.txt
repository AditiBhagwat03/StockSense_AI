POLICY ID: INV-001
TITLE: Inventory Status Classification

Purpose:
Define how inventory coverage is interpreted.

Stock coverage is calculated as:
Days of Cover = Current Stock / Average Daily Sales

Classification:
Healthy: More than 14 days of coverage.
Watch: 7 to 14 days of coverage.
Medium Risk: 3 to less than 7 days of coverage.
High Risk: Less than 3 days of coverage.

If average daily sales cannot be reliably calculated
because insufficient sales history is available, the
system must not assign a stock-out risk level.

The system must show the data used for every
inventory classification.
