POLICY ID: STO-001
TITLE: Stock-Out Risk

A product is considered high priority when its estimated
days of stock coverage is below 3 days.

If the product's supplier lead time is greater than its
remaining stock coverage, replenishment should be
reviewed immediately.

If sufficient sales history is unavailable, the system
must state that stock-out risk cannot be reliably
estimated.

The system must never guarantee that a product will
sell out on a specific date.
