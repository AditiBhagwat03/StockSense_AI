# STOCKSENSE AI

AI-powered retail intelligence for smarter store decisions.

## 1. What is STOCKSENSE AI?

STOCKSENSE AI is a retail sales and inventory copilot designed to help store managers understand what is happening in their stores, identify what needs attention, and decide what to review next.

It analyzes sales, inventory, supplier, and store data to identify:

* Products at risk of stock-out
* Overstocked products
* Sales spikes and sales drops
* High-priority issues
* Supplier lead-time concerns
* Possible inter-store inventory reallocation opportunities

The system combines deterministic business analytics with Gemini-based natural-language responses. Important retail metrics are calculated from verified data rather than generated by the AI.

## 2. Key Features

### Executive Dashboard

Provides a quick view of:

* Total products
* Low-stock products
* Overstock situations
* Sales changes
* Stock-out risk
* Today's priorities

### Stock-Out Risk Detection

Calculates Days of Cover using:

```text
Days of Cover = Current Stock / Average Daily Sales
```

Risk levels are determined using predefined inventory policies:

* Healthy: More than 14 days
* Watch: 7–14 days
* Medium Risk: 3–<7 days
* High Risk: Less than 3 days

Supplier lead time is also considered when identifying urgent replenishment concerns.

### Overstock Detection

Identifies products with unusually high inventory coverage.

The system can flag:

* Very high stock coverage
* Products with stock but no recent sales

It recommends reviewing purchasing, allocation, or promotion decisions rather than automatically assuming that a discount is required.

### Sales Spike and Drop Detection

Compares:

* Current 30-day sales
* Previous 30-day sales

A change of 30% or more is treated as a sales anomaly.

The system identifies:

* SALES_SPIKE
* SALES_DROP

The measured change and underlying sales values are provided to the AI as verified facts.

### What Needs Attention Today?

STOCKSENSE AI combines verified retail signals to create a prioritized list of issues that may require store-manager attention.

### Natural-Language Copilot

Store managers can ask questions such as:

* Which products are at risk of stock-out?
* Should we take action on the Wireless Mouse?
* Which products are overstocked?
* What happened with Wireless Earbuds sales?
* What needs attention today?
* Can we reallocate inventory between stores?

The system also handles unsupported questions without inventing an answer.

## 3. How It Works

STOCKSENSE AI follows a grounded AI architecture:

```text
User
  ↓
Frontend Dashboard
  ↓
FastAPI Backend
  ↓
Intent Detection
  ↓
Deterministic Retail Analytics
  ↓
Local Policy Retrieval
  ↓
Gemini
  ↓
Evidence Validation
  ↓
Structured Response
  ↓
Frontend
```

Important business calculations are performed by Python analytics modules.

The local policy retrieval layer finds relevant policy evidence from the project's policy documents.

Gemini receives the verified analytics and relevant policy evidence and converts them into a clear, structured response.

## 4. Deterministic Analytics

The system keeps business calculations outside the language model.

Python is responsible for:

* Days of Cover
* Stock-out risk
* Overstock status
* Sales percentage changes
* Sales anomalies
* Supplier lead-time alerts
* Priority items
* Inventory reallocation opportunities

This prevents the language model from becoming the source of truth for numerical business metrics.

## 5. Policy-Grounded Retrieval

STOCKSENSE AI uses a transparent local retrieval layer over its own policy documents.

The retrieval system:

* Loads local policy documents
* Splits them into searchable chunks
* Matches user questions with relevant policy content
* Scores matching policy chunks
* Returns the most relevant policy evidence
* Provides policy IDs and source documents to the response layer

No external retrieval service is required.

## 6. Gemini Integration

Gemini is used as the natural-language reasoning and explanation layer.

Gemini receives:

* User question
* Verified analytics
* Relevant policy evidence

It returns a structured response containing:

* Answer
* Verified facts
* Recommended action
* Policy evidence
* Limitations

The model is not responsible for calculating retail metrics.

## 7. Hallucination Prevention

The system is designed to reduce unsupported AI responses.

Key safeguards include:

* Gemini does not calculate business metrics
* Verified analytics are treated as authoritative
* Policy evidence comes from local policy documents
* Unsupported questions are controlled
* Unknown causes are not invented
* Inventory movements are not claimed without evidence
* Purchase orders are never claimed as placed
* Delivery dates are not guaranteed
* Sellout dates are not guaranteed
* Insufficient data is explicitly reported
* Generated policy evidence is validated against retrieved policy IDs

For example, if a product has a 70% sales increase but the dataset does not contain the reason, STOCKSENSE AI reports the measured increase and clearly states that the cause cannot be determined from the available data.

## 8. Dataset

The project uses a fictional retail dataset created specifically for the application.

It contains:

* 4 stores
* 40 products
* 4 suppliers
* 14,400 sales records
* 160 inventory records
* 6 retail policy documents

The sales dataset contains 90 days of retail activity.

The dataset includes normal cases as well as deliberately created scenarios for testing:

* High stock-out risk
* Watch-level stock coverage
* Overstock
* Sales spike
* Sales drop
* Insufficient sales history
* Zero-sales inventory
* Inter-store inventory availability

## 9. Policy Documents

The local policy layer contains:

* Inventory Policy — INV-001
* Replenishment Policy — REP-001
* Stock-Out Policy — STO-001
* Overstock Policy — OVS-001
* Sales Anomaly Policy — ANM-001
* Reallocation Policy — REL-001

These policies provide the rules used to explain retail decisions and recommendations.

## 12. How to Run

### Step 1 — Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/AditiBhagwat03/StockSense_AI.git
cd StockSense_AI
```

### Step 2 — Create a virtual environment

Windows:

```powershell
python -m venv .venv
.venv\Scripts\activate
```

### Step 3 — Install dependencies

```powershell
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

### Step 4 — Configure Gemini

Create a `.env` file in the project root:

```text
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key
```

Do not commit the `.env` file.

### Step 5 — Start the application

```powershell
python app.py
```

The application runs on:

```text
http://127.0.0.1:8000
```

Open the address in a browser to access the STOCKSENSE AI dashboard.

## 17. Project Goal

STOCKSENSE AI aims to make retail decision-making faster and more reliable by combining:

* Realistic retail data
* Deterministic business rules
* Local policy-based retrieval
* Grounded generative AI
* Clear evidence
* Action-oriented insights
